# SHOTLIST — "Pierce The Veil: 20 Years" (Parts 1–3)

Conventions
- 1080×1920, 30 fps, silent. Rendered at 2160×3840, downscaled (lanczos), H.264 CRF 18.
- Every shot: ease-in → move → ease-out → **HOLD ≥ 1 s**. Moves are dolly / crane / orbit, ≤ 15°/s rotation.
- Light rig per shot: **KEY** (warm, shadow-casting, 4096 map) + **RIM** (cool, from behind the subject) + **FILL** (dim). Practicals (lamps, windows, stage lights) are emissive and are the only things that bloom.
- One text card per beat: appears at beat start + 1 s, holds ≥ 3.5 s, max 6 words, lower third on a soft dark gradient (y ≈ 1180–1540), subject stays in the upper two thirds.
- Year tag: top-left (x 60, y 240), taken from the **beat table row**, changes only on a beat cut.
- Safe zone: nothing in the top 220 px, bottom 380 px, right 120 px.

Grades: 04–07 amber/red · 08–10 dusty gold→teal · 12 icy blue/white · 16 sunset orange · 17–21 desaturated blue-grey · 22–23 neon green/black · 25–26 magenta/gold.
