From a suburban garage to a sold-out stadium: the first years of Pierce The Veil. 🎸

SUMMARY
Brothers Vic and Mike Fuentes rename their band Pierce The Veil in fall 2006, after a song on Before Today's 2004 album.
"A Flair for the Dramatic" (2007) is written as a duo and recorded in Seattle, then Jaime Preciado and Tony Perry join to make four.
After a full Warped Tour run in 2008, "Selfish Machines" (2010) hits #1 on Billboard Heatseekers. Part 2 is next.
