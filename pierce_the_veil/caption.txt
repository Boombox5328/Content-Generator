SUMMARY
Two brothers renamed their band in 2006 and grew it, album by album, from Warped Tour to #4 on the Billboard 200.
After a quiet stretch from 2017, The Jaws of Life (2023) brought them back, and "So Far So Fake" blew up in 2025.
The I Can't Hear You World Tour ends at Petco Park in San Diego on Sept 12, 2026.
