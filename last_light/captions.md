## Voice-over script (burned into the mix by `audio.py`, edit lines in `spec.py`)
1. Everything that has ever happened is barely the opening frame.
2. Fourteen billion years. Stars, planets, life, us. That's the whole story so far. Now let's go forward.
3. Our species might last a million years. Maybe less. Maybe far more. But Earth has a deadline either way.
4. In about a billion years, a brightening Sun boils the oceans. In five billion, it swells into a red giant. Then it shrinks into a cooling ember.
5. Our galaxy may merge with Andromeda. And as space expands, every other galaxy slips out of view. Future astronomers would see one galaxy, alone, in an empty sky.
6. The smallest stars burn for trillions of years. Then the last one goes dark. No new stars will ever form again.
7. Galaxies dissolve, flinging dead stars into the void. And if protons decay, even matter itself fades away.
8. Black holes are the last things standing. Over a googol years, even they evaporate, ending in one final flash.
9. (whispered) No light. No heat. Nothing left to happen. This is heat death.
