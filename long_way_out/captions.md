# The Long Way Out — posting copy

**Caption hook**
One family, 10 million years, and an entire galaxy. Where would YOU live? 🚀

**Engagement prompt (pin as first comment)**
Moon, Mars, Titan, or a spinning space city? Comment below.

**Description / disclaimer**
A speculative timeline based on real science and proposed concepts, not predictions.

**End card**
Edit `CHANNEL` in `spec.py` (currently `@YourChannel`) and re-run `build.sh`.

## Voice-over script (edit lines in `spec.py`)
1. So far, every human story has happened on one planet. What if that changes?
2. First, the Moon. Three days away, with ice we can turn into water, oxygen and fuel. It's a gas station for the solar system.
3. Then Mars. Months away, with a thin toxic sky, so we live underground and under glass. And one day, a child is born who has never seen Earth, except as a star.
4. The asteroid belt holds more metal than humans have ever mined. Europa hides an ocean under ice. And on Titan, the air is so thick a person could strap on wings and fly.
5. Eventually we stop needing planets. We build spinning worlds with gravity, sky, and seasons. And around the Sun, a swarm of collectors harvests its power.
6. The nearest star is over four light-years away. A tiny probe might get there in decades. People could take centuries, and the ones who leave won't be the ones who arrive.
7. Star by star, the wave spreads. Even slow ships could reach across the whole galaxy in a few million years. A blink, compared to Earth's history.
8. But there's a limit. Space itself is expanding. Most galaxies are already moving away too fast, so we could never reach them, even at light speed.
9. Everything after begins with the first one.
